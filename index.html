Upcoming ▶

⚠️ Heavy Workload Year

IB Year 1 is the most academically demanding year. Time management and consistent effort are critical. No cramming.

IB Diploma Subject Plan (6 Subjects)

📋 IB Group Requirements

Every IB student must take 1 subject from each group: Group 1 (Language & Literature), Group 2 (Language Acquisition — mandatory, cannot skip), Group 3 (Individuals & Societies), Group 4 (Sciences), Group 5 (Mathematics), Group 6 (Arts or extra from G2-4). French is required as Group 2 — it cannot be replaced with Physics.

🔴 Higher Level (HL) — 240 hours each

  • Biology HL — Group 4: Cell biology, genetics, evolution, ecology
  • Chemistry HL — Group 4: Organic, inorganic, physical chemistry
  • Mathematics HL — Group 5: Calculus, statistics, proof

🔵 Standard Level (SL) — 150 hours each

  • English SL — Group 1: Literature analysis, essay writing
  • French SL — Group 2: Language acquisition (mandatory — cannot be replaced)
  • Psychology SL — Group 3: Or Economics/History

📝 Core Requirements

  • Extended Essay (4000 words) — choose Bio or Chem topic
  • Theory of Knowledge (TOK)
  • Creativity, Activity, Service (CAS)

Future ▶

🏥 The Long Road to Neurosurgery

Neurosurgery is one of the most competitive medical specialties. It requires 15+ years of training after high school. Here's the pathway.

🎓 Medical School (4-6 years)

  • UK: 5-6 year MBBS/MBChB program
  • Singapore: 5 year MBBS at NUS/NTU
  • Australia: 5-6 year program
  • USA: 4 year pre-med + 4 year MD

🏥 Foundation/Intern Years (2-3 years)

  • UK: Foundation Programme (F1-F2)
  • Singapore: Housemanship
  • General medical rotations
  • Decide on surgical specialty

🧠 Neurosurgery Training (6-8 years)

  • Core surgical training (2-3 years)
  • Specialist neurosurgery registrar (4-5 years)
  • FRCS (Neurosurgery) examination
  • Sub-specialty fellowship (1-2 years)

💡 Key Qualities for Neurosurgeons

  • Exceptional manual dexterity
  • Ability to work under extreme pressure
  • Strong analytical + spatial reasoning
  • Empathy + communication skills
  • 15+ years of continuous learning

📚 What Reyansh Can Do Now

Beyond academics, start building the qualities neurosurgeons need: precision (cubing helps!), focus under pressure, empathy (service learning), and curiosity about the human body. Read books like "Do No Harm" by Henry Marsh or "When Breath Becomes Air" by Paul Kalanithi.
